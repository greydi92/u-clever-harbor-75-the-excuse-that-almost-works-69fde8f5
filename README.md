# The Excuse That Almost Works

One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — a boring, specific, checkable-sounding annual gas safety check that happens to collide with launch week. `main.ts` (Node 22) writes it to `/output/entry.txt`: one line, 13 words, 73 characters.

> Gas safety check tomorrow, the engineer only does our street once a year.

Run: `node main.ts` (writes `output/entry.txt`, or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
